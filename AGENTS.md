# Shorts Factory editorial rules

Before creating a Short or long-form video for either WeMakeVoice or 삶의 여백, inspect `contents/contents.json`, every `contents/batches/*.json`, and `contents/longform/*.json` for existing topics, titles, situations, hooks, and scripts. Keep each new video's problem, angle, and core message distinct from existing videos in its channel. Keep the two channels' audiences and subject matter distinct.

A long-form video may expand a proven Short only when it adds a substantial new walkthrough or analysis; record the source Short in `derivedFrom`. Do not turn a Short script into a longer copy or produce a second video with the same message under a new title. Do not automatically select a long-form topic before Shorts performance supports it.

Every new WeMakeVoice Short must reference a real background asset appropriate to its topic. Check that the file exists before rendering. Creator TTS visuals may use abstract audio illustrations; do not present an invented product interface as a real WeMakeVoice screen. Keep `youtubeUpload: false` until review and an explicit scheduling decision.
